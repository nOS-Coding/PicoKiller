How to use

  1. Flash it: hold BOOTSEL while plugging the Pico in, then drag picokiller-pico2-kit.uf2 (firmware + config) onto the drive. Re-flash it whenever you change the config.
  2. Replug the Pico into the victim machine. The LED blinks for the fuse delay, then the pranks fire.

Machine must be yours to prank. Nothing damages hardware.
